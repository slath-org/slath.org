Appearance and time zone

Settings → Appearance holds how Slath looks for you. The choices belong to your account, so they follow you to every device you sign in on.

  • Theme: System (follows your device), Light, or Dark.
  • Density: Comfortable or Compact. Compact has less padding; text and touch targets keep their size.
  • Code font size: 12 to 20 pixels, for code, diffs, and commands.
  • Time zone: the zone pages and emails show times in.
  • Commands for: whether pages show checkout and conflict commands for Git or for jj. Automatic follows what your recent pushes came from. See Merge requests.

Time zones

Until you choose one, times are in UTC. When you open the page, it proposes the zone of the device you're using ("Proposed from this device: Europe/Berlin"); nothing changes until you save. The list is the IANA time zone database built into Slath, by region; Etc holds fixed offsets such as UTC−12.

With a zone chosen:

  • Pages show times in it, correct across daylight saving changes: recent ones as "3 hours ago", older ones as a date ("14 Nov 2026"), with the exact time and the zone's abbreviation or offset in a tooltip ("2026-11-14 10:05 CET", "2026-11-14 14:50 UTC+05:45").
  • Emails Slath sends you, such as security notices, use it too.
  • Dates without a time, such as a milestone's due date, are calendar dates: the same day for everyone, whatever their zone.
  • The API always returns times in UTC (RFC 3339), whatever your setting.
  • Scheduled work, such as an instance's daily backup, runs at a time stated in UTC.
